# How do i load into these files?
it's actually quite simple.
Step 1: download the project by clicking the green code button, then download ZIP
Step 2: extract the rbxl folder from the zip file. move it to any place other than downloads, i recommend documents.
Step 3: to open the levels in roblox studio, click on file, then open from file. navigate to the rbxl folder you just extracted. click the level you wanna open then click on the open button in the left corner.
